# LÖVE Gin Poker

An experimental combination of gin rummy and poker, with the mechanics of gin rummy, but scored by forming poker hands, not melds.

Uses a poker hand, where aces are high, rather than gin rummy, where aces are low. 

Dealership alternates from round to round, with the first dealer chosen by any agreed upon method. The dealer deals seven? cards to each player one at a time starting with their opponent, and then places the next card in the deck face up. This begins the discard pile. The face down pile is known as the stock pile.

- Drag card to discard
- [Play] button (when some cards are selected) (underneath hand) (no discard button)
- [Sort Rank] [Sort Suit] buttons (underneath hand) (maybe - YAGNI)
- Display type of poker hand selected, hand value £, total value £ (where?)
- Tap on stock/discard to draw a card to hand
- Round drawn if stock cards exhausted
- Buttons on the right for right-handed players (could be a setting)
- Add special cards to hand, which increases power but decreases playable hand size, eg stone cards, wildcards, multipliers, &c
